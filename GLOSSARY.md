# Kanban

A board of Cards that move through work statuses.

## Language

**Board**:
The surface that shows Cards grouped by status.
_Avoid_: dashboard, page, list

**Card**:
A work item on the Board.
_Avoid_: ticket, task, issue, note

**Title**:
The name of a Card. It is the text shown on the Card on the Board.
_Avoid_: name, heading, label
